中科大高考采访USTC · Gaokao interview

中国科学技术大学官方微信公众号于 2023 年 5 月 28 日发布的采访记录。An interview published by the University of Science and Technology of China’s official WeChat account on May 28, 2023.

原文摘录：Excerpt translated from the Chinese original:

“考前不怕，考后不悔”，人生不止高考，高考真正的意义在于找到适合自己的路，不要随波逐流。学习力和创新力才是未来社会的核心竞争力。“No fear before the exam, no regrets afterward.” Life is about more than the Gaokao. Its real meaning is finding a path that suits you, rather than following the crowd. The ability to learn and innovate is what will matter most in the society of the future.

朱振方
高考分数：672Zhenfang Zhu
Gaokao score: 672
