哈佛毕业典礼：扎克伯格演讲Zuckerberg’s Harvard commencement speech

中文原文附英文翻译。来源链接指向原始内容。English translation of my Chinese notes. Source links point to the original content.

  • “最伟大的成就往往源于敢于失败的勇气。准备得太久，你就没有办法开始了。”

意义感：超越个人的目标

  • 与人为善的底层逻辑

    扎克伯格哈佛首日穿反衣服却无人提醒，唯独 KX Gin 不在意并邀他合练，此人后来成为 Facebook 重臣。这不仅是趣闻，说明了在尚未成功时，那份纯粹的善意与协作往往能决定未来的高度。

  • 约会背后的“借口”

    即便面临被开除的危机，也能将其转化为约会的浪漫契机。这极致的乐观：没有任何事情是绝对糟糕的，关键在于你如何定义它并借力前行。

  • 从“个人目标”到“众人意义”

    找到个人目标只是起点，真正的挑战是让每个人都找到人生的意义。不仅要创造就业，更要重塑社会的“目标感”，这是当代领导者肩负的最高责任。

行动力：在误解与瑕疵中迈步

  • 实现想法的人就是你

    当你确信一个改变世界的想法如此清晰时，不要等别人出现，那个实现它的人就是你。扎克伯格最初的梦想并非办公司，而是创造影响力。

  • 拒绝“完美成型”的陷阱

    没有人一开始就无所不知，想法并非一开始就完美。如果你因为害怕被误解、怕挨批、怕犯错而止步不前，结果只能是对现状视而可见。

  • 敢于“领先于时代”的代价

    追求远大理想的人常被视为疯子，挑战复杂难题的人会因不全盘掌握而自责，行动太快的人总会挨批。但这都不能成为止步的理由，因为今天做的每一件事，未来都难免出现问题。

体系化：重构社会的生存环境

  • 逻辑反转：投资“预防”而非“治疗”

    当前社会在治疗上的花费是治愈研发的 50 多倍，这完全不合逻辑。如果反过来投资，让人们从一开始就不生病，才是真正的进步。

  • 教育与民主的个性化革命

    推动民主现代化（在线投票）与教育个性化。让每个人在接受基础教育（水的教育）的同时，也能放大其天赋与激情（火的教育）。

  • 失败的“缓冲空间”

    J.K.罗琳被拒绝 12 次才出哈利波特，碧昂丝写了数百首歌才有《Halo》。成功的衡量标准应是“有多少人拥有有意义的角色”，我们应探讨全民基本收入（UBI）等理念，给每个人尝试新想法的自由。

连接力：从社群到全球协作

  • 历史的指向性

    世界历史始终指向更大规模的协作：从部落到城邦，再到国家。当今重大的机遇都具有全球性，需要我们超越个体去实现伟业。

  • 改变始于身边

    能否把握最大机遇，取决于我们构建社群、创造世界的能力。让每个人在生命中不仅因为工作，更因为社群的连接而找到人生目标。

思考点：

  • 如何通过 Redding 让人们在获取知识的同时，更能放大内心的那团“火”？或许“赋予人生意义”正是下一代互联网产品的核心底层逻辑。

  • “The greatest achievements often come from the courage to fail. If you spend too long preparing, you never get to start.”

Purpose beyond personal goals

  • The deeper logic of kindness

    On his first day at Harvard, Zuckerberg wore his clothes inside out, but no one told him. KX Gin was the exception: he did not mind and invited Zuckerberg to work together. He later became a key figure at Facebook. This is more than an anecdote. Before success arrives, simple kindness and collaboration can help shape how far you eventually go.

  • An “excuse” for a date

    Even the prospect of being expelled could become a romantic opening to ask someone out. This is extreme optimism: nothing is entirely bad; what matters is how you frame it and use it to move forward.

  • From a personal goal to shared purpose

    Finding your own purpose is only the beginning. The real challenge is helping everyone find meaning in life. Beyond creating jobs, we need to renew society’s sense of purpose. This is the highest responsibility of leaders today.

Taking action amid misunderstanding and imperfection

  • You are the person who can make the idea real

    When an idea that could change the world seems clear to you, do not wait for someone else to appear. You are the one who can make it happen. Zuckerberg’s original dream was to create an impact, rather than to start a company.

  • Avoid the trap of waiting for a perfect idea

    No one knows everything at the beginning, and ideas do not arrive fully formed. If fear of misunderstanding, criticism, or mistakes stops you, you can only watch the status quo continue.

  • The price of being ahead of your time

    People with ambitious dreams are often called crazy. Those tackling complex problems blame themselves for not understanding everything, and those who move quickly are criticized. None of this should stop us: whatever we do today will inevitably face problems in the future.

Systems that reshape the conditions of life

  • Reverse the logic: invest in prevention rather than treatment

    Society currently spends more than fifty times as much on treatment as on research into cures. That makes little sense. Reversing this investment so that people do not become ill in the first place would be real progress.

  • A personalized revolution in education and democracy

    Modernize democracy through online voting and personalize education. Alongside basic education, the “water” of education, help everyone develop their talents and passions, the “fire” of education.

  • A cushion for failure

    J.K. Rowling was rejected twelve times before publishing Harry Potter, and Beyoncé wrote hundreds of songs before Halo. Success should be measured by how many people have meaningful roles. We should discuss ideas such as universal basic income (UBI) to give everyone the freedom to try new things.

Connection from communities to global collaboration

  • The direction of history

    World history has moved toward collaboration at greater scales: from tribes to city-states to nations. Today’s major opportunities are global. They require us to work beyond ourselves to achieve something significant.

  • Change begins close to home

    Our ability to seize the biggest opportunities depends on our ability to build communities and create a world together. People should find purpose through their connections to communities as well as through their work.

A question to consider

  • How can Redding help people gain knowledge while also nurturing the “fire” within them? Perhaps giving life meaning is a central principle underlying the next generation of internet products.
