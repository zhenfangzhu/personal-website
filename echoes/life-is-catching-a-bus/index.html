人生就是一场赶车Life is a race to catch a bus

中文原文附英文翻译。来源链接指向原始内容。English translation of my Chinese notes. Source links point to the original content.

事情经过

  • 天赋（起点）

    出校门-西北门就是科大东区车站，离得很近。

  • 目标（动力）

    如果没有清晰的目标（赶上 166 路车），那也不会奔跑。

  • 努力（过程）

    我打算去银泰迪卡侬买一件衣服，在东区 1958 咖啡馆忙完事后，看到车距仅剩 1 分钟 2 站。 但是我事已经做完，所以没有选择等下一班的 20 分钟，而是选择立刻起跑。

  • 运气（变量）

    校门口恰好的绿灯，和路口正在等候红灯的 166 路车。

核心思考

  • 如果我不奋力奔跑，那个恰好的绿灯对我毫无意义，它会像以往一样变红。

  • 运气是给赶路人准备的捷径，而不是给观望者预留的空位。

  • 运气负责在那一刻给你机会；而努力，负责让你在那一刻，刚好在场。

What happened

  • Talent — the starting point

    The USTC East Campus bus stop is just outside the northwest gate, very close by.

  • A goal — the motivation

    Without a clear goal, catching the No. 166 bus, I would not have started running.

  • Effort — the process

    I planned to buy a piece of clothing at Decathlon in the Intime shopping mall. After finishing my work at the East Campus 1958 café, I saw that the bus was only one minute and two stops away. Since my work was already done, I chose to start running immediately rather than wait twenty minutes for the next bus.

  • Luck — the variable

    The light at the campus gate happened to be green, while the No. 166 bus was waiting at a red light at the intersection.

The central thought

  • If I had not run as hard as I could, that perfectly timed green light would have meant nothing to me. It would have turned red, just as it always did.

  • Luck is a shortcut for people already on their way, not a reserved seat for those watching from the sidelines.

  • Luck gives you an opportunity at a particular moment; effort makes sure that, at that moment, you are there.
